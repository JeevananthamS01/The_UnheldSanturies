The Unheld corporate difference

Every corporate wellness provider in Chennai offers stress management and mindfulness. Nobody offers guilt counseling for the employee who missed a critical deadline and hasn't forgiven themselves. Nobody offers shame work for the manager who was passed over for promotion and can't shake the feeling they don't belong. We do. That's the gap we fill — and it's the reason our programmes produce lasting change, not just short-term relief.

The 360° school approach — what no one else does

Most school counselors see students only. We work in three rings simultaneously: students (the most visible struggle), teachers (who absorb students' pain and have nowhere to put it), and parents (who often transfer their own unresolved shame onto their children's performance). Healing one ring without the others is like watering a plant while the roots remain dry.

Step-1

We come to your campus

Step-4

Aggregate wellbeing insights for management — themes, patterns, recommendations. No individual student data ever shared.
